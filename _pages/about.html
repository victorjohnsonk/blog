About Victor Johnson's Blog

Where AI Engineering Meets Cybersecurity

Victor Johnson's Blog explores the intersection of artificial intelligence, software engineering and cybersecurity. From building intelligent applications to understanding the risks they introduce, the blog focuses on the technologies, challenges and engineering decisions shaping modern AI systems.

Topics range from generative AI and large language models (LLMs) to retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), AI agents, prompt injection, AI security testing and secure application design. The focus extends beyond how AI technologies work to examine how they can be developed responsibly, evaluated effectively and protected against emerging threats.

Practical Learning, Experiments and Insights

This blog is a space for sharing technical knowledge through hands-on tutorials, engineering walkthroughs, practical experiments and security-focused discussions. Articles aim to connect technical concepts with real implementation challenges, helping readers understand both the possibilities and limitations of modern AI technologies.

Readers can expect explorations of AI architectures, coding techniques, security considerations, development tools and lessons learned along the way. As the fields of AI engineering and cybersecurity evolve, the blog will continue to document new ideas, technical discoveries and approaches to building more reliable and secure AI applications.

The Person Behind the Blog

Victor Johnson is an AI Engineer working in the cybersecurity industry, with a professional focus on building AI-powered applications and systems. His interests include generative AI, agentic workflows, RAG architectures, AI security and the practical challenges of developing trustworthy AI solutions.

The blog reflects an ongoing journey of engineering, learning and exploring the security of AI systems. For more information about his professional background, technical projects and experience, visit Victor Johnson's Portfolio .
